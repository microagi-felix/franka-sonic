### WP 4.2 — `plan/REPORT.md`

One table with a row per lane (and per oracle), columns:

| lane | full-success rate | milestone 1…6 reach rates | median steps-to-success | action jerk | eval wall-clock | GPU-hours |

Then, in prose kept short:

- **Fine-tune parity statement** — the exact command each lane ran (steps,
  batch, GPUs, horizon, action encoding). If the two differ in any way, say so
  at the top; a comparison across different budgets is not a comparison.
- **Oracles** — A-oracle ≈ 100 % is the sanity floor; B-oracle is lane B's
  ceiling. Interpret: a low B-oracle means the controller, not the VLA, lost.
- **Deviations and decisions** taken during P0–P3, pulled from STATUS.md (e.g.
  motion library without human clips, GPUs available, keep-rate).
- **Scale-up deltas** — what a real run needs, itemised: demo count, full
  BONES-SEED retarget, 4096 envs × 8 GPUs for SONIC RL, 20 000 fine-tune steps
  for both lanes, longer eval (≥ 100 rollouts), the real-cell caveat that
  joint-target policies need a joint-impedance controller on the FR3s that the
  repo does not have today.

GPU-hours: derive from the run folders' timestamps and their `config.json`
device lists; if a number is not recoverable, write "not recorded" rather than
guessing.
