### WP 2.5 — motion library (run folder `…_motion_lib`)

   wxyz/xyzw boundary.
2. **Human motion via GMR** (the volume half): clone
   `https://github.com/YanjieZe/GMR` to `~/code/upstream/GMR` and check out

   the hardcoded `choices` list in `scripts/smplx_to_robot.py`. Write
   `ik_configs/smplx_to_dual_fr3.json` — arms-only: position weight on the two
